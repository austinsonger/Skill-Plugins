## Standard inherited citations (most CSPs)

Annex A physical and infrastructure controls are typically inherited from the CSP's own ISO 27001 certificate:
- A.7.1 — Physical security perimeters
- A.7.2 — Physical entry
- A.7.3 — Securing offices, rooms, facilities
- A.7.4 — Physical security monitoring
- A.7.5 — Protecting against physical and environmental threats
- A.7.8 — Equipment siting and protection
- A.7.10 — Storage media (data center portion)
- A.7.14 — Secure disposal or re-use of equipment

Pull the CSP's ISO 27001 certificate and Statement of Applicability (when available) from the attestation portal.

## Annex A theme coverage (ISO 27002:2022 attributes)

ISO 27002:2022 introduced "attributes" for each control (Control type, Information security properties, Cybersecurity concepts, Operational capabilities, Security domains). These are useful for cross-referencing but not required in the evidence matrix.

## Typical extra_xlsx_tabs

- `Statement of Applicability` — list of all 93 Annex A controls with Applicable (Y/N), Reason for inclusion / exclusion, and Implementation status. ISO 27001 requires this document.

## ISO 27001 evidence sourcing principles

- **ISMS context first.** Clauses 4 (context) and 6 (planning) are the foundation. Pull risk assessment and risk treatment plan evidence early — they anchor every Annex A control.
- **Documented information.** ISO 27001 uses "documented information" terminology; the evidence is documents, records, and configurations. Both policy documents AND operational records are required.
- **Internal audit + management review cadence.** Clause 9.2 (internal audit) + 9.3 (management review) must occur "at planned intervals" — typically annually, with documented agenda, attendees, inputs, and outputs.
- **Continual improvement evidence.** Clause 10 requires nonconformities and corrective actions to be tracked with root-cause analysis. Keep the corrective-action register up to date.
- **Annex A control applicability.** The SoA (Statement of Applicability) justifies inclusion or exclusion of every Annex A control. Each applicable control needs operational evidence.

## Citation text source

ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (clauses) and ISO/IEC 27002:2022 (Annex A control implementation guidance). The exact text is copyrighted by ISO and must be paraphrased carefully — quote the standard sparingly and link the user back to the official text.

## Typical evidence retention bullets

- Retain the SoA, risk assessment, risk treatment plan, and ISMS scope statement for the certificate term plus one cycle.
- Retain internal audit reports and management review minutes for the certificate term (typically 3 years).
- Retain training records, access reviews, and incident records as required by the ISMS retention schedule.
