Once the domain is identified, provide a comprehensive guidance session structured as follows:

**1. Domain Overview**
- What this domain covers and why it matters
- How it fits within the SCRMS framework (which Principle(s) it primarily supports)
- The PPTDF dimensions it touches (People / Processes / Technology / Data / Facilities)

**2. Key Requirements**
List all the sub-requirements the entity must address in this domain per SCRMS Section 11, including:
- The requirement statement
- The relevant SCF control ID(s) (e.g., GOV-01, IAC-06, VPM-01)

**3. Evidence Needed to Demonstrate Conformity**
For each key requirement, what evidence must exist to prove the control is implemented and operating as intended? Include:
- Documentation evidence (policies, plans, records)
- Technical evidence (logs, scan results, configurations)
- Operational evidence (meeting minutes, training records, test results)

**4. Common Gaps**
What do organizations most commonly get wrong or miss in this domain? Include:
- "Security theater" patterns (documented but not operational)
- Control drift scenarios (it worked once, now it doesn't)
- Missing evidence pitfalls
- Governance failures (nobody owns it)

- Level 5 (Optimized) — continuously improved
